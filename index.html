🚗 CBR Theory Course

Seven narrated lessons for the Dutch car theory exam (English, 50 questions, pass 44/50). Headphones recommended — each lesson is ~8–10 minutes.

DAY 1
Priority & Right-of-Way
Right-before-left, shark's teeth, the priority signs, roundabouts, trams — the 5-step ladder.
DAY 2
Signs & Road Markings
The shape+colour system: triangle warns, red circle forbids, blue circle commands, blue square informs.
DAY 3
Speed Limits & Zones
The default ladder 50/80/100/130, the daytime-100 motorway rule, zones, the erf, lowest number wins.
DAY 4
The Other Road Users
Cyclists vs your right turn, zebra rules, snorfiets/bromfiets, tram priority, the truck's dode hoek.
DAY 5
Alcohol, Drugs, Documents & Fines
0.5‰ vs the 0.2‰ novice limit, zero-tolerance drugs, the phone rule, WA insurance and APK.
DAY 6
Parking, Lights & the Car Itself
Stopping ≠ parking, the unsigned 5-metre bans, the blue disc zone, rear fog under 50 m, 1.6 mm tread.
DAY 7
Insight, Anticipation & Full Recap
How the exam thinks: may/wise/must, the four staged hazards, the whole week on one wall.
